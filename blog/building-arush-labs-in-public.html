What's live, what's roadmap — the honest version

By Arush Kumar · August 2026 · 5 min read

Arush Labs started as a rule before it started as a company: never let a "coming soon" product look finished. Every page on our site labels exactly what's live, what's in active development, and what's still a roadmap item — because I've seen too many small AI studios sell a demo as a product. That's the fastest way to lose a client's trust, and trust is the only thing a five-person team from Kanpur has to compete with bigger agencies.

What's actually live today

SecureAI Agent is our first fully shipped product — an AI firewall that screens uploads before they ever reach a model, catching OCR-based prompt injection and hidden instructions in images. It's not a prototype; it's running today.

Alongside it, our voice agent and chatbot demos are live and callable — the same agents we'd deploy for a clinic, salon, or real estate office are the ones a visitor can test on our site right now.

What's in active development

ThreatShield AI — real-time cybersecurity threat detection on network traffic — is being built and tested against real traffic patterns before we call it done. It's further along than a prototype but I won't call it "live" until I'd trust it on my own infrastructure.

What's still roadmap

Our flagship, Arush Marketplace — an AI-powered freelancing ecosystem where an AI co-founder turns a one-line brief into a scoped PRD and a starter code scaffold — is targeting a Diwali 2026 launch. OfficeOS, our AI office-automation product, and ideas like an AI CoFounder and Arush Launch, are next in line. None of them are for sale yet, and none of them appear on our pricing page pretending otherwise.

A roadmap item sold as a shipped product is a debt you pay back in refunds and reviews. We'd rather grow slower and keep every client's trust intact.

Why this matters if you're hiring us

If you come to Arush Labs for a voice agent, a chatbot, a website, or automation, you're working with a small team — Yogita on operations, Aniket on engineering, Adeel on finance, Ayushi on growth, Abhay on business — that ships directly, without outsourcing your project to a subcontractor you'll never talk to. Free consultation first, transparent starting prices, and a straight answer about what's real.
