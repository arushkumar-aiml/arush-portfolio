Why I'm betting on AI voice agents for small business

By Arush Kumar · August 2026 · 6 min read

Every missed call at a clinic, salon, restaurant, or real estate office is a lead that just went to a competitor. That single fact is why AI voice agents are the first thing Arush Labs builds, ahead of chatbots, ahead of dashboards, ahead of anything else on our roadmap.

A chatbot waits for someone to open a website and type. A voice agent answers the phone the way a business already expects to be reached — at 11pm, on a Sunday, during a rush when every staff member is busy. It books the appointment, answers the FAQ, qualifies the lead, and hands off to a human only when it genuinely can't help.

Where it actually pays off

Not every business needs one. But a specific set of businesses lose real revenue to missed calls: clinics and hospitals booking appointments, salons and gyms managing walk-in demand, restaurants taking reservations during peak hours, and real estate and construction offices fielding the same five questions from every caller. These are the industries where a 24/7 answering agent isn't a novelty — it's the difference between a booked slot and a lost customer.

How we price it, honestly

We built three tiers because a single clinic's front desk and a multi-location hospital's call center are not the same problem: a Starter tier for a single location, a Professional tier for growing teams, and an Enterprise tier for organizations running the agent across multiple numbers and workflows. No custom quote required just to see a number — the pricing is public, and there's a live demo you can call before you commit to anything.

The businesses that benefit most from a voice agent are the ones whose phone rings more often than their team can answer it — not the ones chasing a trend.

What I tell every business I talk to

Start with the call volume you already have. If most inbound calls are the same three or four questions — hours, pricing, availability — a voice agent pays for itself inside the first month. If your calls are mostly complex, one-off conversations, you need a human, and I'll tell you that directly instead of selling you an agent you don't need.
